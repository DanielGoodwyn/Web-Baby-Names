Baby Names USA • Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Names USA ("we", "our", or "the app"), created by Daniel Goodwyn, handles your information when you use our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and only collect information essential for the app's functionality:

  • Account Information: If you create an account or sign in with Google or email, we store your user ID and email address to authenticate your session.
  • Saved Names & Favorites: The baby names you star or save to your "My Names" list are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore so they remain synchronized across your devices.
  • App Usage Data: We do not track your location, browsing habits, or personal communications.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Provide core application features (saving and displaying your favorite baby names).
  • Maintain and authenticate your user account.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers to power app infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase & Firestore: Provides secure authentication and cloud database services. For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties or data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove saved names at any time directly within the app by clicking the delete icon next to any saved name. If you would like your account and all associated data permanently deleted, contact us at the email address below and your request will be honored promptly.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
